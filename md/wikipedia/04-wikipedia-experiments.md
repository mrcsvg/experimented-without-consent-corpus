Give our experiments a go!
The Wikimedia Foundation has been testing new ways of making human knowledge even more accessible. Known as experiments, these short, time-bound projects help us better understand how Wikipedia can reach new audiences, respond to global trends, and meet the evolving needs of internet users. (Keep checking back for updates.)
