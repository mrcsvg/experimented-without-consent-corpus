A/B Testing
Last updated: 5 months ago
As an advertiser, it’s important to achieve maximum value and return on investment (ROI) for your ad campaigns, understand what resonates with your audience, and optimize your campaigns based on statistically significant data. You can achieve this using A/B Testing, which compares the performance of two ad sets with separate budgets that differ by one variable.
A/B Testing supports both Classic and Accelerate ad sets, which means you can test a Classic ad set against an AI-powered Accelerate ad set to determine ad set impact. A/B Testing for Accelerate is available for ad sets that meet our A/B test requirements.
Important to know
Due to privacy regulations, when targeting in the European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland, cost per paid conversion and cost per qualified lead metrics will only include data from members who have given explicit consent.
Please note, A/B Testing temporarily restricted targeting members in the EEA and Switzerland. For any test targeting these regions between October 8, 2023 and February 5, 2024, we retroactively re-processed the test results and filtered out actions from members in the EEA and Switzerland who have chosen not to connect their Marketing Solutions services to their core LinkedIn experience. Learn more about member options for connecting to LinkedIn services .
How A/B Testing works
A/B Testing helps you to achieve a higher return on investment (ROI) by measuring the performance of each strategy on a cost-per-result basis, and as a result, guides you to meaningful next steps. Depending on the ROI and key performance indicators (KPI), for example, cost per click (CPC), Campaign Manager determines a winner or confirms that the difference between both ad sets is negligible.
A/B tests cannot be activated the same day they are created. When the end date of an A/B test is reached, the campaign running the test will display the A/B test status. For example: Active, Paused, or Ended.
Important to know
Conclusive results aren't guaranteed.
Examples of A/B tests for Classic and Accelerate ad sets
Example one - Classic ad sets. You've two identical ad sets (ad set one and ad set two) except for the creative content (image A and image B) you want to test with your target audience.
Example two - Classic versus Accelerate ad sets. You’ve created a Classic ad set with a brand awareness, website visits, engagement, video views, lead generation, or website conversions objective that you want to test with your target audience. But you also want to test how it performs against a similar AI-powered Accelerate ad set. The only difference between the ad sets is the creative content (image A and image B) you want to test with your target audience.
For both examples, the audience is split evenly into two separate groups for the duration of the A/B test. Group one is only exposed to ad set one, and group two is only exposed to ad set two.
Ad set one is only shown image A, and ad set two is only shown image B. The winning ad set is the ad set that drives the lowest cost per KPI.
Important to know
• Some members might appear in both the test and control groups, but our data and platform checks have shown that the effect is generally insignificant.
• To maximize result reliability, an A/B test and a Brand Lift test can't run simultaneously in the same account.
